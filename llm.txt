## 2. THE THREE NODES: ROLES, OBJECTIVES & INNER MODULES

### NODE 1: Alpha Forge Sim Lab Server (`Alpha Forge Sim Lab Server`)
* **Primary Role**: The Fleet CIO, Research Lab, and Strategy Orchestrator.
* **Environment**: Runs on Jetson Orin Nano, port `4000`. Publicly accessible via Cloudflare Tunnel at `https://simlab.measmony.me`.
* **Execution Mode**: **PAPER TRADING ONLY**. Never signs real mainnet transactions. It generates zero-risk forward testing data, monitors macro regimes, backtests challenger hypotheses, and pushes optimal calibrations to live clients.
* **Core Subsystems**:
  1. `src/pipeline/alpha-producer.ts`:
     - Compiles the global `AlphaBundle` every cycle.
     - Injects market regime (`RANGING_CHOP`, `TRENDING_EXPANSION`, `HIGH_VOLATILITY`).
     - Injects **Pillar 1 Dynamic Maker Offsets** (e.g. 1.2 bps on BTC, 2.2 bps on SOL, 3.6 bps on TAO), with 20% tightening during chop.
     - Controls asset-specific cooldowns (`coolOffActive: true` when win rate < 40%).
     - Directional locks (`bannedSide: 'SHORT' | 'LONG' | 'BOTH'`).
  2. `src/pipeline/client-registry.ts`:
     - Manages `data/mainnet-clients-registry.json`.
     - Tracks connected clients, heartbeat timestamps, balances, and PnL.
     - Supports per-client strategy assignment (`assignedStrategyId`) and custom pair overrides (`clientPairOverrides`).
  3. `src/pipeline/license-manager.ts`:
     - Generates and verifies cryptographic connection tokens (`simlab_live_...`).
     - Enforces tiered access (`7d_trial`, `lifetime`, whitelisted on-chain desks).
  4. `src/learning/shadow-arena.ts` & `src/learning/hypothesis-engine.ts`:
     - Runs 24/7 automated strategy tournaments.
     - Tests challenger variants (e.g., widening Breakeven Ratchet ATR clearance) against the active champion without risking real capital.
  5. `src/macro/*`:
     - Feeds real-world macroeconomic data (FRED API, BLS inflation, CryptoQuant exchange reserves, Treasury yields, RSS News Agent) into the World State Orchestrator.
  6. `src/market/discovery.ts`:
     - Continuously scans Decibel DEX contracts on Aptos to detect newly listed perp pairs and measure liquidity.
  7. `src/market/regime-classifier.ts` (**NEW — Institutional 4-Regime Classifier**):
     - Classifies every monitored asset into one of 4 quantitative market regimes every 30 seconds:
       * `REGIME_STRONG_TREND`: ADX ≥ 25, EMA20/50 directional separation (BULLISH or BEARISH).
       * `REGIME_RANGE_ROTATIONAL`: ADX < 20, flat Bollinger Bands, price oscillating between VAH/VAL.
       * `REGIME_HIGH_VOLATILITY`: ATR expansion ratio ≥ 1.6×, RVOL ≥ 2.0×, or imminent macro news.
       * `REGIME_COMPRESSION_SQUEEZE`: John Carter BB inside Keltner Channels, NR7, or Inside Bar pre-breakout.
     - Uses EMA(20) and EMA(50) separation for trend direction (BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL).
     - Detects **NR7** (current candle range narrowest of last N bars) and **Inside Bar** patterns for compression.
     - Maps each regime to optimal **Strategy Blueprints** (e.g. `SETUP_1_VWAP_EMA_PULLBACK`, `SETUP_9_CARTER_BB_KELTNER_SQUEEZE`).
     - All thresholds fully mutable via `regime-tuning.ts` (zero hardcoded numbers).
  8. `src/market/volume-profile.ts` (**NEW — Auction Market Theory Volume Profile Engine**):
     - Computes full institutional-grade Volume Profile from 15m OHLCV klines:
       * **POC** (Point of Control): Price node with the highest traded volume.
       * **VAH / VAL** (Value Area High/Low): Boundary containing the tunable % (default 70%) of total session volume.
       * **Value Area Width %**: `(VAH - VAL) / POC × 100`.
       * **LVNs** (Low Volume Nodes): Volume troughs < 25% of POC volume — liquidity vacuum / slippage accelerators.
       * **Developing POC (d-POC) Trend**: Tracks POC migration across session thirds (`STEPPING_UP`, `STEPPING_DOWN`, `STATIONARY`).
       * **Virgin POCs (vPOCs / Naked POCs)**: Previously untouched POC levels from past sessions; stored in `data/vpoc-store.json`.
     - Volume is distributed into `numBins` (default 80) price bins using proportional overlap weighting — not naive close-price bucketing.
     - vPOC lifecycle: auto-registers session POC daily, marks vPOC as `TESTED` when any subsequent kline traverses the level.
  9. `src/market/regime-tuning.ts` (**NEW — Autonomous Regime Tuning Config**):
     - Single source of truth for all Regime Classifier and Volume Profile numeric thresholds.
     - Persisted to `data/regime-tuning.json`; survives container restarts.
     - Autonomously mutated by `autoTuneFromLesson(lesson, outcome)` closed-loop learning loop.
     - All mutations are clamped within safe mathematical bounds and versioned by `tuningGeneration`.
     - Can be manually edited via `POST /api/sim/regimes/tuning`.
  10. `src/market/regime-service.ts` (**NEW — Background Regime Scan Coordinator**):
     - Background service that calls `classifyAssetRegime()` every 30 seconds across all 15+ default watch pairs.
     - Caches results in `regimeCache: Map<string, RegimeClassification>`.
     - Exposes `getClassification(symbol)` and `getAllClassifications()` to `alpha-producer.ts` and API routes.
  11. `src/api-server.ts` & `ecosystem.config.js` (**Sim Lab UI Load Memory Stabilization & Proxy Armor**):
     - Expanded PM2 `max_memory_restart` from 850M to 1500M with `--max-old-space-size=1536` on Jetson Orin Nano (4.2GB free RAM), preventing premature process termination during UI dashboard SSE streaming and concurrent initial state loads.
     - Accelerated V8 garbage collection interval from 5m down to 60s (`global.gc()`), keeping heap usage lean (~270MB–320MB).
     - Added `res.headersSent` guards in `json()` and `setCors()`, and hardened the `/aptos-node/...` proxy error handler against `ERR_HTTP_HEADERS_SENT` uncaught exceptions during network resets or socket cancellations.
     - Debounced disk cache writes in `savePort3000Telemetry()` (3s throttle) to prevent file system I/O thrashing during client telemetry bursts.

   12. `src/bykaranteli/client.ts` (**NEW — ByKaranteli Institutional Microstructure & Multi-Venue Liquidity Engine**):
      - Ingests high-resolution order-flow and liquidity data from ByKaranteli institutional endpoints (`llms.txt` tools):
        * **18-Band Multi-Venue Liquidity Maps** (`/api/liqmap/public`): Overhead squeeze resistance walls, downside flush walls, and primary price magnets.
        * **Hyperliquid Top-1000 Whale Positions** (`/api/public/whales`): Notional USD size, distance to liquidation %, and nearest whale liquidation price.
        * **Market Pressure Metric** (`/api/public/pressure`): 0–100 net directional force scoring with regime labels ("Net short bias building", etc.).
        * **VPIN Order-Flow Toxicity** (`/api/public/flow`): Volume-Synchronized Probability of Toxicity, tracking 90-day percentile toxicity and p90 threshold breach.
        * **Cascades & Incident Circuit Breaker** (`/api/public/incidents`): Real-time multi-venue liquidation cascade tracker, logging forced order bursts ($136M+ flushes).
        * **Whale Aggressor Tape** (`/api/public/whales`): Live prints of $1M+ aggressive taker market orders with aggressor side.
        * **Auction Market Theory Market Profile** (`/api/public/market-profile`): Daily naked POCs (Point of Control) and value area migration.
      - **Quota Preservation & Poller Strategy**: Staggered background polling every 3.5 minutes with 180s in-memory TTL caching (`memoryCache`). Consumes ~8,000 requests/month against the 15,000 monthly quota.
      - **Zero Client Overhead**: Pre-computes `executionDirectives` (`suggestedTp1Price`, `suggestedSlPrice`, `vpinToxicity`, `cascadeActive`) and broadcasts them downstream to the fleet inside `/api/pipeline/alpha-bundle` (`pairDirectives[sym].bykaranteli`). Connected clients consume 0 external API calls.

## 5. PILLAR 1: MAKER-FIRST POST-ONLY ROUTING SPECIFICATION (VERIFIED)

* **Goal**: Completely eliminate taker fee drag (~$35-40 drag across 384 trades that flipped +$41 gross profit into -$16.33 net PnL) by strictly executing Maker PostOnly orders (`TIF = 1`).
* **Offset Calculation**:
  - **Tier-1 Majors (BTC, ETH)**: `0.00010` to `0.00015` (1.0–1.5 bps). Tight priority.
  - **Liquid Tier-2 (SOL, APT, SUI)**: `0.00020` to `0.00028` (2.0–2.8 bps).
  - **Volatile Alts (TAO, HYPE, ENA, etc.)**: `0.00035` to `0.00050` (3.5–5.0 bps). Adverse selection shield.
  - **Regime Tightening**: When `regime === 'RANGING_CHOP'`, offsets tighten by **20%** to guarantee orders sit at the inside quotes.
* **Execution Flow on Clients (Client V1, Client V3, Sim Lab)**:
  1. Client determines order side (`buy` / `sell`).
  2. Queries Sim Lab `pairDirectives[symbol].makerOffsetPct` (0.00010 to 0.00075 dynamic offset).
  3. Computes resting limit price: `isBuy ? markPrice * (1 - offset) : markPrice * (1 + offset)`.
  4. Submits on-chain with `TimeInForce = 1` (`Post-Only Maker`) via Decibel DEX Move entrypoint `dex_accounts_entry::place_order_to_subaccount`.
  5. **Zero-Taker Fallback Enforcement**: When `strategy.layer5.enforcePostOnly` is active (`true` across all institutional templates):
     - Orders crossing the spread are rejected by the matching engine with `OrderEvent` `status: 'CANCELLED'`. Client immediately records zero-fee clean cancellation without triggering IOC market order fallback.
     - Resting orders wait up to `makerFillTimeoutMs` (25000ms). If unfilled within the window, the client cancels the resting order on-chain.
     - **Taker fallback is strictly blocked** (`allowTakerFallback: false`). No taker fees (5-6 bps) are ever paid on entry or exit, preserving 100% of trading edge.

### C. Volume Profile Engine — Key Concepts

| Concept | Description | Field in Schema |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **POC** (Point of Control) | Price level with the highest accumulated volume — the fair-value anchor | `poc` |
| **VAH** (Value Area High) | Upper boundary containing 70% (tunable) of total session volume | `vah` |
| **VAL** (Value Area Low) | Lower boundary of the 70% value area | `val` |
| **Value Area Width %** | `(VAH - VAL) / POC × 100` — measures how wide or narrow consensus is | `valueAreaWidthPct` |
| **LVN** (Low Volume Node) | Price trough where volume < 25% of POC volume — a liquidity vacuum / slippage accelerator | `lvns[]` |
| **vPOC** (Virgin / Naked POC) | Past session POC levels that have never been retested — act as magnet targets | `activeVpocs[]` |
| **d-POC Trend** | Developing POC migration direction across session thirds | `dProcTrend` |

**Volume distribution method**: Volume is distributed into `numBins` (default 80) price bins using proportional overlap weighting across each candle's high–low range — not naive close-price bucketing. This produces realistic Auction Market Theory profiles from free public OHLCV data.

**vPOC lifecycle**: Registered daily to `data/vpoc-store.json`. Automatically marked `TESTED` when any subsequent kline traverses the POC level. Up to 20 historical session records kept per symbol.

### M. Client V1 Sniper Elevation: 1H Box POC Retest, Counterfactual Veto, Structure-Break Cuts & Dynamic Trailing Stop (2026-09-30)

#### 1. Operational Problem & Root Cause Audit
* **Symptom**: Client V1 (Live Mainnet, Jetson Port 3000) suffered significantly larger average drawdowns (-4.5% to -7.5%) compared to Client V3 (-1.0% to -1.9%).
* **Root Cause 1 (Intraday Churn & Chasing)**: Client V1 entered trades on short-term 15m momentum spikes and breakout candles without waiting for an established consolidation box or value retest.
* **Root Cause 2 (No Pre-Trade Simulation Filter)**: Client V1 lacked Client V3's counterfactual check, entering assets even when Sim Lab paper simulations showed <35% win rate.
* **Root Cause 3 (Holding Through Structure Breaks)**: Client V1 lacked `DEFENSIVE_STRUCTURE_BREAK_CUT`, holding losing positions through 15m CHoCH/BOS reversals all the way to maximum SL.
* **Root Cause 4 (Suppressed Trailing Stops)**: `initialRisk` in `index.ts` was anchored to `hardStopLoss` (15% margin risk ceiling) instead of true soft SL, preventing trailing ratchets from activating.

#### 2. Architectural Invariants & Upgrades Implemented

##### Component 1: 1H Box Consolidation & Volume Profile POC Retest Setup
* **Files**: `alpha-autonomouse-client_v1/src/market/candlestick.ts` & `src/market/signals.ts`
* **Logic**:
  - `detect1hBoxConsolidation(klines1h, currentPrice)`: Scans 4–8 hour horizontal compression coils (≤3.8% range) and computes auction Point of Control (POC) and 70% Value Area (`VAH`/`VAL`).
  - **Breakout Anti-Chasing Gate**: Flags `WAITING_FOR_POC_RETEST` upon breakout (chasing capped at 55% veto).
  - **Retest Continuation Trigger**: Unlocks a +15% confluence bonus only when price pulls back within ±0.35% of the Box POC with ≥25% rejection wick and 1H trend alignment.

##### Component 2: Sim Lab Counterfactual Pre-Trade Paper Veto

### O. Dynamic Per-Client Strategy Assignment Architecture & Fleet Consensus (2026-09-30)

#### 1. Architectural Answer: Dynamic Per-Client vs. Fleet-Wide Strategy
* **Question**: Is each client dynamically assigned its own strategy, or do all clients run the same strategy based on the code?
* **Answer**: **Each client desk is dynamically and individually assigned its own strategy.** They are NOT hardcoded or forced to run the same strategy.
* **Mechanism**:
  1. Each registered desk in `data/mainnet-clients-registry.json` maintains its own `assignedStrategyId`, `autoMatchRiskSentinel` flag, `harvesterProfile`, and `clientPairOverrides`.
  2. When an execution desk polls for its Alpha Bundle (`GET /api/pipeline/alpha-bundle?clientId=<CLIENT_ID>`), `alpha-producer.ts` invokes `getClientActiveStrategy(clientId)` from `src/strategy/manager.ts`.
  3. `getClientActiveStrategy(clientId)` verifies that the client's assigned strategy is either certified by RiskSentinel or staged in the Ready Arsenal, and returns that specific desk's configuration.
  4. In addition to distinct strategies, each desk receives:
     - **Client-Specific Harvester Parameters**: Unique `vulnerabilityHarvestThreshold`, `accelerateBreakevenR`, and `minHarvestNetPnlPct` tailored to account size and risk appetite.
     - **Client-Specific Pair Overrides**: Individual asset leverage, stop loss multipliers, and confidence gates.

#### 2. The Three Autonomous Governance Tiers (`auditAndRebalanceClientStrategies()`)
The rebalance loop in `src/pipeline/client-registry.ts` classifies and monitors desks across three separate governance tiers:

| Tier | Example Node | Governance Rules & Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Flagship Core Anchor** (`isCoreAnchor: true`) | `desk-01-jetson` (Client V1, Port 3000) | Protected by a **24-hour / 10-trade hysteresis anti-whipsaw lock**. Evaluated via `evaluateCoreAnchorRotation(client)`. Only rotates if rolling execution degrades (<45% win rate over 10 closed trades) or a sustained 4H macro regime shift occurs. |
| **Tier 2: Agile Satellites** (`autoMatchRiskSentinel: true`) | `v3-2d90f27f06e3` (Finland VPS), `v3-524f67edcdf8` (Alpha 3) | **Individually monitored on every cycle**: If Satellite A underperforms (<45% WR over 10 trades), *only Satellite A* rotates to a superior Arsenal alternative. If Satellite B suffers setup starvation (0 trades in ≥ 4 hours on low-frequency templates), *only Satellite B* rotates to an active hunter strategy. |
| **Tier 3: Manual Pinned Desks** (`autoMatchRiskSentinel: false`) | Any desk manually locked via UI or API | **Immune to automated rotation**. Retains its assigned strategy indefinitely regardless of regime shifts, macro changes, or champion promotions. |

#### 3. Why the Fleet Temporarily Synchronizes to a Single Champion
When the AI Research Engine promotes a new champion strategy (e.g. `ai_strategy_1790750000000` — "6-Pillar Fleet CIO V13") through the 5-checkpoint RiskSentinel Consensus Gate, all desks configured with `autoMatchRiskSentinel: true` are synchronized to the champion to eliminate execution drag across the fleet. However, once live, each desk's subsequent tenure, degradation checks, and potential rotations operate completely independently.

### R. Client V3 Risk Guardrails Hotfix: Budget Ceiling ($200), Max Position ($60), Max Concurrent Positions (3) (2026-09-30)

   - UI guardrails ($60 Max Position, 3 Concurrent Positions) were written to `settings.json` but omitted from `configSchema` and `guard.ts`.
   - Sizing defaulted to `maxBudget * 35%` ($70–$105 margin), and concurrent open positions were unconstrained.
3. **No On-Chain Duplicate Check**:
   - Out-of-sync local cache allowed repeated order placement for the same symbol on Decibel DEX.

#### 3. Applied Solution & Verification
1. **Executor Maker Fill Polling (`src/trades/executor.ts`)**:
   - Fixed condition to `pos.sizeBase > 0 || pos.sizeUsd > 0 || pos.size > 0`.
   - Added just-in-time fill recovery after `cancelAllOrders`.
2. **Hard Margin Clamps (`src/risk/guard.ts`)**:
   - Enforced `targetAllocUsd = Math.min(targetAllocUsd, userMaxPosUsd, remainingBudget)`.
   - Guaranteed single order margin $\le \$60$ and total deployed $\le \$200$.
3. **Concurrent Position Veto (`src/risk/guard.ts` & `src/trades/executor.ts`)**:
   - Enforced immediate rejection if `openTrades.length >= config.MAX_OPEN_POSITIONS` (3).

### 3. Operational Signal Definitions & Mathematical Rules

Every operational term utilized across the trading system is strictly defined by deterministic mathematical rules and quantifiable data sources:

#### A. Absorption Wick
* **Data Sources**: 15m OHLCV Candlestick Feed + Decibel Order Flow Volume.
* **Operational Formula**:
  - Candle Range = High - Low
  - Lower Wick % = ((min(Open, Close) - Low) / Candle Range) * 100
  - Upper Wick % = ((High - max(Open, Close)) / Candle Range) * 100
* **Absorption Rule**:
  - **Bullish Absorption**: Lower Wick % >= 40% AND RVOL >= 1.5x (Relative Volume) AND CVD Slope is ACCUMULATION (Passive buyers absorb aggressive market selling).
  - **Bearish Absorption**: Upper Wick % >= 40% AND RVOL >= 1.5x AND CVD Slope is DISTRIBUTION (Passive sellers absorb aggressive market buying).

#### B. Shift of Dominance / Micro Structure Shift (MSS / CHoCH)
* **Data Sources**: Rolling 20-bar 15m Fractal Swings (Williams Fractals N=2).
* **Operational Rule**:
  - **Bullish MSS / CHoCH**: Price closes strictly above the most recent confirmed Fractal Swing High (Close_t > SwingHigh_{t-1}) following a downtrend sequence of Lower Lows, accompanied by an expanding green candle (Body > 60% of total range).
  - **Bearish MSS / CHoCH**: Price closes strictly below the most recent confirmed Fractal Swing Low (Close_t < SwingLow_{t-1}) following an uptrend sequence of Higher Highs, accompanied by an expanding red candle.

#### C. Confirmed Retest vs. Failed Retest
* **Confirmed Retest**: After breaking structure, price pulls back into the broken level (or dynamic EMA 25/50 / FVG zone), touches the level within +/- 0.20%, and prints a candle closing away from the level in the breakout direction within <= 2 bars.
* **Failed Retest**: Price re-enters the broken structure and prints a full candle body close back inside the prior range (Close < Level for Longs, Close > Level for Shorts), signaling a false breakout / liquidity trap.

#### D. Real Limit Wall vs. Order Book Spoofing
* **Data Sources**: Top 5-10 levels of the Decibel DEX L2 Order Book (`mcpClient.getOrderBook()`).
* **Operational Metrics**:
  - Imbalance % = ((Bid Depth USD - Ask Depth USD) / (Bid Depth USD + Ask Depth USD)) * 100
* **Distinction Criteria**:
  - **Order Book Spoofing**: |Imbalance %| > 65% located within 0.3% of mark price that is cancelled or disappears in < 2,000 ms upon price approach, without corresponding trade volume prints on the tape.
  - **Genuine Limit Wall**: Substantial depth that persists for > 30 seconds and absorbs trade prints (demonstrated by high traded volume at the limit price without the price advancing through it).

#### E. Trapped Participants
* **Data Sources**: Coinglass Open Interest (dOI_24h) + Mark Price Change (dPrice_24h).
* **Operational Classification**:
  - **Bull Trap (Short Squeeze Exhaustion)**: dPrice > +2.0% while dOI < -2.0%. Rally is fueled strictly by shorts closing (covering), with zero new spot/perp buyers entering. Trade is vetoed.
  - **Bear Trap (Long Squeeze Flush)**: dPrice < -2.0% while dOI < -2.5%. Dump is fueled strictly by cascaded long liquidations. As liquidations exhaust, passive buyers absorb supply. Shorting is vetoed.

### 6. Small Sample Variance Safeguards & Wilson Confidence Intervals

To avoid overfitting to short winning streaks, the Promotion Gate (`promotion-gate.ts`) enforces the **Wilson Score 95% Confidence Interval**:

#### Mathematical Formula:
Given w wins out of n closed shadow trades, sample win rate p_hat = w / n. With standard normal quantile z = 1.96 (95% two-sided confidence):
- p_lower = (p_hat + z^2 / (2n) - z * sqrt((p_hat * (1 - p_hat) / n) + z^2 / (4n^2))) / (1 + z^2 / n)

#### The 5 Statistical Hurdle Criteria:
1. **Minimum Sample Size**: N >= 25 closed shadow trades.
2. **Expectancy Ratio**: E_chal >= max(0.35R, E_champ * 1.15).
3. **Win Rate Floor**: p_hat_chal >= 55%.
4. **Max Drawdown Ceiling**: MDD_chal <= max(5%, MDD_champ * 1.10).
5. **Wilson 95% Lower Bound Floor**: p_lower >= 42.0%.
*This prevents a candidate with 5 wins out of 6 trades (83% nominal win rate, but Wilson lower bound of only 43.6% with N=6) from being promoted until sufficient sample size confirms statistical significance.*

## SECTION XIV: ON-CHAIN TRADE HISTORY ALIGNMENT & 1M/3M STRUCTURAL SWEEP UPGRADE (2026-10-20)

### 1. On-Chain Ledger & UI History Reconciliation Architecture
* **The Problem**:
  - The Client V3 UI History page showed optimistic simulated results (e.g. SOL/USD `entry: $105.80`, `exit: $105.89`, `+$0.30`, `status: CLOSED_TP`), while the actual on-chain fill on Decibel DEX was `CloseLong @ $105.77`, realized PnL `-$0.3320`, fee `$0.3162`, net loss `-$0.6482`.
* **Root Causes Identified & Fixed**:
  1. **Premature Software Exit Attribution**: In `trades/executor.ts:monitorOpenTrades`, when live ticker price crossed the target, the in-memory engine immediately stamped `status = 'closed_tp'`, `exitPrice = currentPrice`, and `pnl = estimatedPnL`, persisting these to SQLite *before* broadcasting the close order to Decibel DEX.
  2. **Status Filter Exclusion in Reconciler**: In `db/database.ts:syncOnChainTrades`, close fills were matched against SQLite using `WHERE status IN ('OPEN', 'open')`. Because the trade was already prematurely marked as `CLOSED_TP`, the query skipped the active trade and mistakenly bound the close fill transaction version (`7695182867`) to an older trade record from October 5th.
  3. **Missing Symbol Resolution in MCP Trade History**: Decibel MCP returns trade history objects with a contract `market` address (e.g., `0xdf3f9...`) without a human-readable `symbol`. In `database.ts`, missing symbols fell back to `'DEX'`, breaking trade matching queries.
  4. **MCP Trade History Timeout**: `getTradeHistory(50)` defaulted to a 2,500ms timeout, which frequently timed out over remote RPC indexer queries, returning empty arrays (`[]`).
* **Engineering Solutions Implemented**:
  1. **Robust Multi-Priority Trade Matcher**:
     - Priority A: Active trades with `status IN ('OPEN', 'open')`.
     - Priority B: Recently closed trades within a 10-minute window (`ABS(closed_at - timestamp) < 600,000ms`), ensuring trades closed by in-memory monitors are immediately rectified with true on-chain fill prices and PnLs.
     - Priority C: Un-reconciled trades on the same symbol within a 24-hour window.
  2. **Authoritative Ledger Overwrite**:
     - When an on-chain close fill arrives, it is the **unconditional single source of truth**:
       `exit_price = oct.price`, `realized_pnl = oct.realized_pnl_amount`, `fee_usd = roundTripFee`, `status = (netPnl > 0.005 ? 'CLOSED_TP' : 'CLOSED_SL')`, `exit_reason = (netPnl > 0.005 ? 'TRAILING_TP' : 'ON_CHAIN_SL')`, and `tx_version = oct.transaction_version`.
  3. **Authoritative Market-to-Symbol Resolution**:
     - Enhanced `mcpClient.getSymbolForMarket` to dynamically resolve all contracts registered in `AUTHORITATIVE_DECIBEL_MARKETS`.
  4. **Expanded MCP Timeout**:
     - Increased `getTradeHistory` timeout to **15,000ms** to guarantee complete indexer synchronization.
